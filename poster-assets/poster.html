The game

A two-player, non-contact boxing match. Each player wears an IMU on both wrists and an AprilTag on the chest, and holds an iPhone. Throw a real jab, hook or uppercut across the room: your phone draws the fight over the camera view, and the Ultra96 decides if it landed.

$1$Gear up
Strap on both gloves, pin on the tag, open the app.
$2$Fight
Punch, block, weave. A hit counts only if your phone sees the tag.
$3$Win
Take them to $0$ health, or lead after $90\,\text{s}$.

Features

  • Real punchesNo controller. Your wrists are the input.
  • Both handsLeft and right punches are told apart.
  • AI on the FPGAA $5$-bit CNN in hardware names each move.
  • Fair by designOnly the Ultra96 sets health and stamina.
  • AprilTag lockYour phone tracks the opponent you aim at.
  • Floor arena + HUDHealth, stamina, timer and hit effects.
  • HapticsThe glove buzzes when you are hit.
  • PickupsHealth and attack boosts appear on the floor.
  • EncryptedTLS from glove to Ultra96, WSS to phones.
  • ReconnectsA dropped glove or phone rejoins the match.
  • TutorialLearn each move before you fight.
  • Headset modeSlot the phone in for hands-free AR.

The move set

$10$ moves, $11$ classes. The CNN tells left from right, so a straight, hook or uppercut each comes in two classes.

An idle class soaks up footwork, so it never fires a punch. Everyone starts with $100$ health and $100$ stamina.

The checksum is the XOR of every byte from type to payload end. The parser knows each type’s length, so it resyncs after a corrupt byte.

CommsAINormalise and quantise

$q_c = \operatorname{clip}\!\left(\left\lfloor 2\,\dfrac{x_c-\mu_c}{\sigma_c} \right\rceil,\,-16,\,15\right)$
Each of the $12$ channels uses the mean $\mu_c$ and spread $\sigma_c$ of the training data. Every scale is a power of two, so the FPGA rescales with bit shifts.

AIEngineWindow to action

$\hat{y} = \arg\max_k\, p_k,\quad p_{\hat{y}} \ge 0.60$
A punch spans $4$ to $6$ windows, so one event fires per $150\,\text{ms}$. Block is sent as start and end, ending after $2$ quiet windows.

EngineVizDamage rule

$D = D_{\text{base}} \cdot k \cdot \begin{cases} 0.3 & \text{blocked} \\ 1 & \text{otherwise} \end{cases}$
$k$ scales with punch intensity. If the attacker’s phone cannot see the tag, the punch is a miss and deals $0$.
03

Four subsystems

What each part does, and what it measured.

Comms

tls, wss, json

Encrypted, self-healing links from glove to phone.

  • Binary glove protocol: handshake, heartbeat, clock sync, actuate
  • A glove silent for $3.5\,\text{s}$ is dropped, then rejoins with a new handshake
  • Phones get a full MatchState after every event and once a second
  • Every attack has an ID, so a retried message is applied once
$\text{TLS}\ 1.2$minimum on both the glove link and the phone link

Hardware

ESP32, MPU6050

Two gloves per player, no room sensors.

  • $6$-axis IMU on each wrist: $\pm 2\,g$ accel, $\pm 250^{\circ}/\text{s}$ gyro
  • Left glove relays through the right glove over ESP-NOW
  • Only the right glove joins Wi-Fi, so each player needs one TLS session
  • Vibration motor at about $70\%$ PWM, pulsed by the Ultra96
$100\,\text{Hz}$sampling on every wrist, $12$ channels per player

Visualizer

Unity 6, ARKit, iOS

The fight, seen through your phone.

  • AprilTag on the chest locates the opponent; the pose is filtered to stay steady
  • Floor detection anchors the arena and the pickups
  • HUD with health, stamina, timer and link status; hit and block effects with sound
  • Read-only: it shows MatchState and never sets damage
$\le 8$pickups on the floor at once, owned by one player each

04

Design choices

The decisions that shaped the system.

EngineViz
One authorityThe game engine runs on the Ultra96. A phone only reports whether it can see the opponent, so lost tracking or a slow phone can’t change the score.
HWComms
Gateway gloveThe left glove talks only to the right one over ESP-NOW. That gives one TLS handshake and one clock per player, so both wrists line up in time.
AI
Gate, don’t voteOverlapping windows see the same punch $4$ to $6$ times. A $0.60$ confidence gate and a $150\,\text{ms}$ refractory make it fire once.
AIFPGA
Powers of twoEvery quantiser scale is $2^{-n}$, so rescaling is a bit shift, not a multiply. That leaves the DSPs for the convolutions.
