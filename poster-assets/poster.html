02

Four subsystems

What each part does.

Hardware

ESP32, MPU6050

Two gloves per player and nothing installed in the room.

  • $6$-axis IMU on each wrist, sampled at a fixed $100\,\text{Hz}$
  • Left glove relays through the right glove over ESP-NOW
  • Vibration motor can be triggered from the Ultra96
DesignOnly the right glove talks to the Ultra96, so each player needs one secure connection.

Comms

TLS, WSS, JSON

Encrypted, self-healing links from glove to phone.

  • Compact binary glove protocol: handshake, heartbeat, clock sync
  • One full-duplex secure WebSocket per phone
  • Sequence numbers and state snapshots survive drops and reconnects
DesignVersioned JSON schemas let the Unity and Python sides test against the same contract.

AI

Brevitas, HLS, Vivado

Recognises moves from the glove data, on the FPGA.

  • Six moves: straight, hook, uppercut, block, weave and pickup
  • Left and right versions of a punch count as one move
  • Looks at $320\,\text{ms}$ of both wrists at a time, with weights stored in $5$ bits

Visualizer

Unity 6, ARKit, iOS

Your opponent, your arena, through your phone camera.

  • AprilTag finds the opponent and floor detection places the arena
  • Live HUD: health, stamina, timer, connection
  • Gloves, hit and block effects, punch sounds
DesignThe phone only displays the game, so lost tracking cannot change the score.
